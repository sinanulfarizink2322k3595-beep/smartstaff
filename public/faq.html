What is SmartStaff? +
SmartStaff is a web-based staff and student management system designed for educational institutions. It streamlines outpass requests, staff availability tracking, meeting scheduling, and administrative management.
How do I register as a student? +
Go to the Student tab on the login page and click "Register here". Fill in your name, email, roll number, department, and password. After registration, verify your email using the link provided to activate your account.
How do I submit an outpass request? +
After logging in to your student portal, navigate to "Outpass Request" in the sidebar. Fill in the reason, destination, and travel dates, then submit. The HOD (Dr. Archana) will review and approve or reject your request.
Who approves outpass requests? +
Outpass requests are reviewed and approved by the Head of Department (HOD), Dr. Archana. You can track the status of your outpass from "My Outpasses" in your student portal.
How do I schedule a meeting with a staff member? +
Log in to your student portal and go to "Meeting Request". Select the staff member you want to meet, choose a purpose from the dropdown, and pick your preferred date and time. The staff member will confirm or reject your request.
How can I see staff availability? +
In the student portal, navigate to "Staff Availability" to view a chart and detailed schedule of when each staff member is available. This helps you choose the right time to request a meeting.
How does a staff member update their availability? +
Staff members can log in to the Staff Portal and navigate to "My Availability". They can check/uncheck morning and afternoon slots for each day of the week (Monday–Saturday) and save their schedule.
What are the default availability hours? +
By default, all staff are available in the afternoon (14:00–17:00) on Monday through Saturday, and in the morning (09:00–12:00) on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.
What can admins do in SmartStaff? +
Administrators have access to the Admin Portal where they can view statistics, manage staff and student records, review all outpass requests and meeting schedules, and read submitted feedback.
How do I submit feedback? +
Visit the Feedback page from the login screen. You can submit feedback anonymously or with your contact details. Rate your experience with stars and provide a message. All feedback is reviewed by the administrator.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. All passwords are securely hashed using bcryptjs. Authentication is handled via JWT tokens that expire after 24 hours. Your credentials are never stored in plain text.
What if I forget my password? +
Please contact your system administrator to reset your password. Future versions of SmartStaff will include a self-service password reset feature via email.
